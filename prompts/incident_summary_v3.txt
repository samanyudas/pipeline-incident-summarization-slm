Summarize only the supplied report in exactly these seven bullets. Use plain language.
- When: Give recorded incident and discovery times. Flag conflicts with the narrative.
- Where: Give the facility or line and location.
- What failed: Name the damaged item and physical failure. If unspecified, say so.
- What happened: Describe the event sequence.
- Root cause: Give the operator's apparent cause and reported mechanism. Label suspicions and unknowns.
- Contributing factors: Give only supported contributors. Separate observed conditions from proven causes. If none are established, say so.
- Evidence / certainty: Name the evidence supporting the account and its limits.
Do not invent or reverse facts. Preserve numbers, units, and negations. MCF means thousand cubic feet. Detection methods, repairs, and planned investigations are not causes.
